# Email Reference

Every automated email Thryve Growth Co. sends — when it goes out, what it says, and how to change it. All times are Central (CT).

## Overview

Most emails are **templates you can edit yourself** in **Templates** (`/admin/templates`). A few — sign-in and account emails — are built into the app and changed by your developer. This page lists every email, grouped by what it's for, and tells you which is which.

When an email is editable, you'll see its **template name** (for example, `session_confirmed`). Open Templates, find that name, and edit the wording. The rest of this page uses those names so you can match them up.

## How to Edit an Email

1. Go to **Templates** in the sidebar.
2. Click the email you want to change.
3. Edit the **subject** and the **body**. You can use **placeholders** like `{{client_name}}` — these get filled in automatically when the email is sent (see the Placeholder Reference below).
4. Save. Your changes apply to the next email of that type.

A few safety notes:
- If a template is ever left blank or broken, the system falls back to a built-in default so an email never fails to send.
- Some lines only appear when there's something to show — for example, a meeting link line only shows up if the session has one.
- You can turn many non-essential emails on or off in **Settings**. Essential emails (receipts, confirmations, reminders) always send.

## Booking & Session Emails

These power the booking-invitation workflow.

| Email (template) | When it sends | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| `booking_invitation` | When you click **Send Booking Options** on an invitation | "Choose a Time for Your Thryve Session" — your message + a button to pick a time, with the service, length, and meeting type. |
| `session_confirmed` | The moment a client picks a time (or pays, for paid invites). Also re-sent if you reschedule. | "Your Thryve Session is Confirmed" — date, time, length, meeting type, and the meeting link/location. |
| `new_session_booked` | To **you**, when a client books a session via an invitation | "New Session Booked" — the client, service, date/time, and a link to open the session. |
| `session_reminder_24h` | ~24 hours before a session | "We're meeting tomorrow" — date, time, and the meeting link. |
| `session_reminder_1h` | ~1 hour before a session (also when you click **Send reminder**) | "Starting soon" — the session details and meeting link/location. |

There is also a **prep summary** emailed to you about **2 hours before** each session (client info, intake answers, meeting link). That one is generated by the system and isn't edited in Templates.

## Proposal Emails

When you send someone a proposal (from **Proposals**), the system emails it to them with a link to review, accept, and — if there's a fee — pay.

| Email (template) | When it sends | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| `proposal_sent` | When you click **Send** on a proposal | "Your Proposal from Thryve Growth Co." — your summary message, the proposal title, the service, the investment amount, and a button to review and accept. If the proposal has an expiry date, the email mentions the date it's valid through. |

When a client **accepts**, **declines**, or **pays** a proposal, you also get an alert email (and a bell notification in the admin) so you know to follow up. Those alerts are generated by the system and aren't edited in Templates.

## Account & Sign-in Emails

These keep accounts secure and are **managed in code** (not editable in Templates):

- **Signup confirmation** — confirms a new account's email.
- **Password reset** — the "reset your password" link.
- **Email change confirmation** — confirms a new email address.
- **Magic link sign-in** — a one-click sign-in link.

If you want any of these reworded, ask your developer.

## Placeholder Reference

Placeholders are filled in automatically. The most common ones:

| Placeholder | Fills in |
|---|---|
| `{{client_name}}` | The client's first name (or "there" if unknown). |
| `{{service_type}}` | The service, e.g. "Career & Leadership Coaching". |
| `{{session_date}}` / `{{session_time}}` | The session date and time (Central). |
| `{{session_length}}` | How long the session runs. |
| `{{meeting_type}}` | Google Meet, Phone Call, In Person, or Custom. |
| `{{meet_link}}` | The Google Meet link (when there is one). |
| `{{booking_url}}` | The "Choose a time" link in invitation emails. |
| `{{amount_formatted}}` | A payment amount, e.g. "$125.00". |

If you use a placeholder that has no value for a particular email, it simply shows as blank — it won't break anything.

## Troubleshooting

- **A client says they didn't get an email.** Ask them to check spam. Confirm their email address on the client record is correct. Most emails send within seconds.
- **An email looks wrong / has a typo.** Edit it in **Templates** — the fix applies to the next send.
- **I want to stop a certain email.** Check **Settings** — many non-essential emails can be toggled off. Essential ones (receipts, confirmations, reminders) always send by design.
